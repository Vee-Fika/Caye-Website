The Technology

300+ patents.
One perfect cup. Every time.

Your team sees a button. Behind it, three technologies work invisibly to deliver barista-quality coffee — every single cup. See the patents →

01

BionicBarista

Emulates the artistry of elite baristas. Each phase — grinding, weighing, tamping, extraction — is meticulously orchestrated to deliver a consistently refined cup.

02

Closed-Loop System (CLS)

The first genuine mechanism for coffee extraction quality control. Automatically monitors and regulates extraction time, coffee dose, and espresso weight in real time.

03

3rd-Gen Precision

Unlike 1st-gen (time-based) and 2nd-gen (thickness-based) machines, CAYE measures powder weight directly via a built-in scale module — accuracy no predecessor can match.
